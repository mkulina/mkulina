## AI project concepts

The following examples explore how I would apply RAG to financial research, healthcare operations, and developer tooling. The statistics are illustrative benchmark targets for proposed implementations.

### Financial document intelligence

A research workspace that compares public financial disclosures and presents generated answers alongside the supporting source passages.

**Illustrative targets**

* Index 10,000 public financial documents with company and reporting period metadata.
* Retrieve relevant evidence in the first five results for 90% of benchmark questions.
* Support at least 95% of sampled factual claims with accurate source citations.
* Reduce median research time from 20 minutes to 5 minutes, a 75% reduction.

**Proposed stack:** TypeScript, Python, Amazon Bedrock, and AWS storage.

### Healthcare operations knowledge assistant

A policy knowledge assistant that helps staff locate procedures, compare revisions, and access documents according to their permissions.

**Illustrative targets**

* Search 2,000 versioned policy and procedure documents.
* Retrieve relevant policy evidence in the first five results for 90% of benchmark questions.
* Reduce median policy lookup time from 8 minutes to 2 minutes, a 75% reduction.
* Pass 500 access control scenarios with zero unauthorized disclosures within that test set.

**Proposed stack:** TypeScript, Python, Azure AI Search, and authenticated document retrieval.

### RAG evaluation platform

A developer workbench that compares retrieval strategies and makes the tradeoffs between answer quality, response time, and cost visible.

**Illustrative targets**

* Compare 12 pipeline configurations against 500 versioned benchmark questions.
* Improve evidence retrieval from 75% to 90%, a gain of 15 percentage points.
* Reduce average answer cost by 30% while maintaining agreed quality thresholds.
* Keep retrieval latency below 500 milliseconds at the 95th percentile under a defined test load.

**Proposed stack:** TypeScript, Python, an optional Go job service, and GitHub Actions.
